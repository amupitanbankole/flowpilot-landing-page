# FlowPilot — AI Workflow Automation Landing Page

A polished portfolio landing page concept for an AI workflow automation product.

## Portfolio objective
Demonstrate conversion-focused landing-page design, visual hierarchy, product storytelling, responsive UX, and the ability to communicate a complex AI product in a few clear steps.

## Design direction
- **Audience:** operations, sales, marketing and product teams
- **Primary conversion:** start a free trial
- **Secondary conversion:** book a demo
- **Visual language:** clean SaaS UI, soft neutrals, electric-violet accent, high-contrast product dashboard
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk + DM Sans

## Page architecture
1. Sticky navigation
2. Value-led hero with product UI preview
3. Social proof and customer logos
4. Feature/value section
5. AI workflow example
6. Three-step process
7. Customer testimonial
8. Final conversion CTA
9. Responsive footer

## UX decisions
The hero answers **what it is, who it helps, and why it matters** before asking for action. The dashboard acts as immediate visual proof rather than decorative imagery. Repeated CTAs are placed after moments of understanding, and the process section reduces perceived complexity from "AI automation" to three approachable actions.

## Interaction details
- Responsive mobile navigation
- Scroll reveal transitions using IntersectionObserver
- Reduced-motion support
- Dashboard CTA micro-interaction
- Hover elevation on product/feature cards
- Animated final CTA visual

## Tech
HTML5 · CSS3 · Vanilla JavaScript

## Design feedback lens
When reviewing this concept, the key questions are: Is the value proposition understood in five seconds? Does the dashboard establish product credibility? Is the page hierarchy guiding the eye toward the trial CTA? Are interactions subtle enough to support, rather than distract from, conversion?
